Tuko pamoja

Support and safe trading

Keep communication clear and check sellers before making payments.

💬 Buyer and seller support

This demo does not have live private messaging. You can use the seller's provided contact details to discuss the product.

Confirm the product, quantity, location and final price. Ask for current photos where appropriate. Agree on delivery and payment terms clearly. Avoid sharing passwords or PINs, and be careful with requests to pay unfamiliar accounts in advance.

🛡️ Buy with care

A listing on this demo is not independently verified. Inspect goods where possible and confirm important details with the seller.

  • Check quality and quantity.
  • Agree on transport costs.
  • Compare offers from several sellers.
  • Keep your transaction records.
